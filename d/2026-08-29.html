SINGA-SPIRES . 18 December 2541

All Bankers Promoted, Locked Out

Trading across the Singa-Spires ocean platforms ground to a halt today after every bank employee reached the rank of Managing Director. The title surge left no staff with the low-level clearance required to unlock the trading floors.

By mid-morning, three hundred senior executives stood on the steel gantry outside the central vault, waiting for a clerk who did not exist.

Personnel files show the firm spent two centuries handing out titles instead of pay increases. The last junior worker was promoted to Senior Executive Director of Water Procurement five months ago. Under platform safety rules, senior ranks cannot override lower-tier locks, a measure built to stop high-level fraud.

"We have forty Vice Chairmen of Risk sitting on the stairs," said Senior Executive Managing Director Haruki Chen over the wind. "None of us has badge clearance for the washrooms, and my rank legally forbids me from holding a mop."

Offshore credit flows froze by noon, stopping bulk grain shipments across three sea sectors. Three thousand executives spent the afternoon sharing muesli bars on the footbridge while cargo barges drifted away below.

The board convened on the landing pad to hire a new junior clerk on the spot. The attempt failed when the panel reflexively promoted the applicant to Vice President before she touched the pen, locking her on the roof with the rest of management.
