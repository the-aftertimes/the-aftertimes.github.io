Kuolema Deep . 28 July 2137

Heat Pump, 212, Demands Afternoon Naps

The Resident Count for Kuolema Deep has named the central heat pump as the settlement's eldest citizen, at 212 years, and has relabelled its maintenance outages as protected naps. Final figures follow.

Total residents counted: 4,118, plus one pump. The next eldest is a retired ice-cutter, aged 97. Residents over 90 number 31, and over 200 number one.

At the birthday party, attendance was 4,118 of 4,118, with no absences to excuse. That included 212 residents who attended while on shift in the pump hall. The cake was 40 metres long and laid along the main gallery, and the air office advised against any flame.

The pump was logged as sleeping for 1,460 hours this past year, the same 1,460 hours once logged as outage. Residents lodged 63 complaints about sleeping hours, and all 63 were upheld. Of these, 58 came from people who had knocked on the casing during a nap.

Four cold-bunk wards lost heat during a nap this winter, and 611 residents were moved into their neighbours' bunks. Nine repairs have been deferred, and every repair is now booked for waking hours only. The ice-cutters on the morning shift must now ask the pump whether it is awake before they ask it for anything.

The next nap is the fourteenth, 0200 to 0600, to be rescheduled if the pump is feeling unwell.

The assembly chair, Anouk Ezeani, said the count had no authority to remove the nap. "It was always asleep. We have simply started to say so, and in fairness it is the only one of us who has never once been late to a shift."
