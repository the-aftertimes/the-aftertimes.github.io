Nimbus Reach . 28 April 4494

Election Day Dies, Beaten By Football

Election Day, the oldest fixed date on the calendar of Nimbus Reach, died this month at 2,014 after the league moved the Skyhook Cup final onto it. It is survived by Vote Week, which nobody likes.

The founding assembly set the day as the third Gale Day of the quiet season, when the platform's winds were mildest. For twenty centuries it passed without incident. Citizens walked to the ballot galleries in their best wraps, and the tally was read from the east rail at dusk.

The final, between the Deckhands and the Lower Rafts, was fixed three years ago. The assembly chair was asked whether the two events could share a day. She said they could not, as the stadium holds the whole city and the galleries hold forty.

The vote was held in the stadium after the trophy ceremony, on the seats. Turnout was 100 percent, the first in the city's history. The returning officer said everyone had been counted at the gate.

Seamus Ahlgren, who has run the east rail tally for thirty years, said the result was valid. "The sheets came out with the cold drinks and most came back in the cups," he said. "You can still read a ballot with a bit of lemon on it. It's the Deckhands' votes I worry about. They were mostly used for a goal."

The Deckhands won the cup. The sitting assembly won the vote, by a margin close to the Deckhands'.

Ahlgren will read the tally from the east rail at dusk on Gale Day anyway. He says someone has to tell the wind.
