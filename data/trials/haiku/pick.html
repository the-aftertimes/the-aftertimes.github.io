The Aftertimes

Carrow Shelf · 2877

  1. the cloud licence clerk
    stamps the thunderstorm for noon
    dry rain takes two weeks

  2. four soft velvet hounds
    queued to have their barking trimmed
    now they hum in C

  3. hall sixteen was sold
    to a soap firm for nine months
    walls smell like fresh foam

  4. fourth ward went to sleep
    twenty minutes ahead of
    its computed turn

  5. pace arch scans the stride
    sets the step count on your badge
    adds two steps for boots

  6. bench form gets approved
    holds three hips but only two
    have the sitting pass

  7. meal cube gets its crest
    stamped with this week's sponsor code
    melts the lemon taste

  8. bunk pad fills with gel
    stiffens at the seventh hour
    rolls the tenant out

  9. gray card turned to teal
    gives the right to take the lift
    only coming down
