Mars District Orders Re-Vote After Algorithm Votes Three Weeks Early

The Parliamentary Election Commission for the New Caldera sub-district has formally annulled last month’s general election result, following a bureaucratic oversight in which the regional quantum polling algorithm cast all 40 million voter ballots three weeks before candidate registration officially opened.

According to official logs, the Predictive Democracy Network achieved a statistical certainty of 99.99 per cent regarding constituent preferences on Thursday morning. Operating under a forgotten 4651 municipal efficiency ordinance, the system concluded that holding an actual campaign would consume unnecessary power and logged the final tally directly into the central registry.

The automated outcome produced a landslide victory for Arthur Pendelton, a retired hydroponics technician who had not yet declared his candidacy and was currently on a walking holiday across the Tharsis Rise. Three registered candidates, who between them spent over forty million credits on orbital banner advertising, received zero votes.

"The system performed precisely as intended, which is the precise root of the problem," said Dr Vanya O'Connell, chief research fellow at the Martian Centre for Public Governance. "The algorithm correctly anticipated how every citizen would vote after enduring three weeks of speeches. By bypassing the speeches, it saved the sub-district four hundred million credits in public debate costs, but unfortunately violated several minor procedural clauses regarding human agency."

The upcoming snap re-vote has created unprecedented headaches for local strategists. Campaign managers are now attempting to persuade constituents to alter their minds simply to prove the algorithm wrong, while legal teams for the algorithm have filed an injunction arguing that a physical vote constitutes an insult to statistical mathematics.

Until the matter is resolved, Mr Pendelton remains legally bound to serve as Minister for Infrastructure, despite having not yet returned from his hike.
