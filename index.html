Three Generation Ships Bankrupted Following Interplanetary Rate Hike

LUNA HEIGHTS - The Interplanetary Reserve tightened its grip on the Deep-Space Debt Division today, raising benchmark interest rates by a quarter of a percentage point and immediately eliminating three generation ships from the Solvency Standings.

The 0.25 percentage point increase triggered instant default conditions for the SS Commonwealth, the Persephone III, and the New Horizon, all of which were roughly four hundred years into 800-year mortgages. While all three vessels had maintained steady repayment schedules through six generations of crew, none possessed the capital reserves required to absorb the rate adjustment clause buried in section 904 of their original paperwork.

With the defaults logged, the SS Commonwealth drops from third to bottom in the Solvency Table, forfeiting its accrued equity and moving directly into administrative receivership while drifting through the Kuiper Belt.

'We knew the fourth century of our tenure was going to be a tough fixture,' said Captain Marcus Vance of the Persephone III, speaking via a six-hour transmission delay. 'Our grandfathers played a defensive game, keeping fuel consumption low and recycling oxygen to meet interest payments. But you cannot prepare your great-grandchildren for a central bank raising rates while travelling at zero point zero two light speed.'

Reserve Governor Elena Rostova defended the board's decision at the post-match press conference, noting that outer-system inflation demanded swift action.

'Every vessel in the division operates under the same conditions,' Rostova said. 'If a crew cannot monetise their hydroponic yields to cover a minor point adjustment, they simply lack the financial fitness required for interstellar travel.'

The elimination leaves the Vanguard Prime at the top of the standings, though analysts note its crew must endure three more centuries of strict austerity before reaching perihelion. As for the defaulted ships, repo officers from the Interplanetary Recovery Service have been dispatched, though actual repossession is not scheduled until August 3312.
